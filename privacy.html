Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. This is a draft for review, not legal advice.

What we collect

When you submit the inquiry form, we receive the details you provide: your name, company, email, the product category you are interested in, and your message. We do not ask for payment information through this website.

How we use it

We use the information only to reply to your inquiry, prepare a quotation, and follow up on the products you asked about. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

Cookies

This site uses minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages help buyers most. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time without losing access to the content.

Data retention

Inquiry records are kept only as long as needed to serve the relationship. If you want your submitted details removed, email bob@ssmug.com and we will delete them.
