Our Factory

Built Like a Factory, Not a Trading Desk

SSMug is run by Yongkang STWADD Houseware Co., Ltd. — a factory-backed manufacturer, not a reseller. We run multiple automated production lines with robotic assistance, and keep mold making, logo printing and assembly in-house.

That is how we quote factory-direct prices with no middleman markup, hold 25–35 day lead times, and ship 500K+ units every month to buyers across 50+ countries. (8+ years, since 2017.)
