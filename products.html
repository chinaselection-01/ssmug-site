Materials & Build

How Our Drinkware Is Made

Most SSMug bottles use food-grade 304 stainless steel (18/8) with double-wall vacuum insulation that keeps drinks cold up to 24 hours and hot up to 12 hours. Lids and seals are BPA-free. Every batch passes AQL inspection before it leaves the factory.

🌡️

Vacuum Insulation

A sealed double-wall vacuum slows heat transfer — no condensation on the outside.

🪙

304 Stainless Steel

Food-grade, rust-resistant and tasteless — the industry standard for reusable bottles.

🔍

AQL Inspection

Acceptance sampling on every order so defects stay below agreed levels.

🎯

Leak-Proof Lids

Silicone seals and secure threads keep bags dry in transit.
