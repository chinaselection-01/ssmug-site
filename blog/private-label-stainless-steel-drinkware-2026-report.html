That is good news for price and speed — and bad news for buyers who cannot tell a factory from a broker. 1. Why Yongkang is the default sourcing base

Three structural advantages keep Yongkang at the center of drinkware sourcing: deep sub-supply chains (steel, vacuum furnaces, coating, printing, packaging all within an hour), fast tooling (molds turned in days, not weeks), and competitive labor and logistics concentrated around Ningbo and Shanghai ports. The flip side is a long tail of intermediaries — which is why the rest of this report matters more than the headline price.

5. Quality-control checklist

The difference between a factory and a broker shows up here. Before you commit, confirm these are spelled out in writing:

  • AQL standard for final inspection (commonly 2.5 AQL for major defects).
  • 100% leak test with a pressure gauge, not visual-only checks.
  • Vacuum performance verified per batch (e.g. 95°C water, 6h, retention figure stated).
  • Food-contact certificates for steel and plastic parts (lid, gasket, straw).
  • Drop / durability test protocol for the finished, packaged unit.
  • Carton quality rated for the destination market's handling.

7. FAQ

What is the typical MOQ for custom drinkware?
For ODM (factory-designed models from our 167+ catalog) MOQ is often 500–1,000 pcs per SKU. For OEM (your own design, new mold) it is typically 1,000–3,000 pcs per SKU. Exact figures depend on capacity, finish and decoration; we confirm per inquiry.

How long does private-label production take?
Sampling is usually 5–7 days; mass production runs 25–35 days after deposit, with rush options available. Shipping adds transit time to your port (typically FOB Ningbo or FOB Shanghai).

Which certifications do you hold?
SGS, TÜV, ISO 9001, FDA, LFGB and BPA-Free. Compliance files for both stainless steel and plastic components are supplied with every shipment.

Can I mix models in one container?
Yes. A 20ft or 40ft container can be mixed across models and categories as long as each SKU meets its MOQ.
