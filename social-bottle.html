The Idea

A Bottle That Carries Your Identity

People already carry a bottle everywhere. We turned that bottle into a shareable business card. Each Social Bottle ships with a passive NFC chip encoded to a unique URL (ssmug.com/bottle.html?sn=SMG000123). The owner taps once to claim it and set a public profile; anyone else taps the bottle with their phone to see that profile and save the contact. It's the cheapest possible "social layer" you can bolt onto drinkware — a sticker and a web page.

Honest Tech

“Can Two Bottles Swap Numbers Just by Touching?”

✅

What the standard bottle does

Each bottle has a passive NFC tag — no power, no chip that thinks. A phone is the reader. You tap your phone to the other person's bottle; their profile opens and you save their contact. Both people tap each other's bottle and you've exchanged details in ~3 seconds each. The social result is exactly “cups meeting = contacts swapped.”

⚠️

Literal cup-to-cup, no phone

A passive tag cannot read or talk to another tag — it has no battery and no reader. True “bottle touches bottle and they auto-exchange” needs a powered smart bottle: NFC controller + BLE + coin-cell/rechargeable + certification (FCC/CE). That's roughly 30–50× the cost of a sticker. We can build it as a premium v2 SKU, but it is not the cheap route.

Bottom line: with NFC tags you get the same human outcome — “碰一下杯就加好友” — at a fraction of a cent of added hardware. The phone is just the messenger.

How It Works

Four Steps, Zero Friction

We Embed & Encode

Each bottle gets an NTAG215 sticker under the base + a laser-etched serial (SMG000123). The tag points to ssmug.com/bottle.html?sn=….

Owner Claims It

Owner taps their own bottle once, fills a 20-second form (name, city, socials, bio), and sets privacy.

Friend Taps

A new friend brings their phone to the bottle. The owner's public profile opens in the browser — no app.

They Connect

They tap “Add to Contacts” (vCard download) or “Say hi” (message to the owner). Done.

Why This Route Is Cheapest

Passive NFC > Smart Module

Passive NFC tag

~$0.15–0.40/unit in volume. No battery, no charging, no firmware. Lasts the life of the bottle. This is the Social Bottle standard.

QR fallback

Even cheaper: print the serial/QR on the bottle for $0 hardware. NFC just removes the “open camera” step and adds the magic story.

Powered smart module

~$8–15/unit (NFC+BLE+battery+cert). Only for a premium v2 where buyers specifically want no-phone auto-swap.
