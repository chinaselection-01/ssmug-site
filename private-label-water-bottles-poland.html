Why Poland

Bottles Polish Buyers Actually Want

Polish retail and e-commerce favor durable, gift-ready reusable bottles. EU rules require food-contact compliance (LFGB / EC 1935/2004), which our SGS/TÜV-tested 304 stainless steel meets. We also exhibit at HOMELIFE Warsaw, so Polish buyers can inspect finish and lids in person.
