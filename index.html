Why Yongkang

The Drinkware Capital of East Asia

SSMug is based in Yongkang, Zhejiang — a city known across the industry as China's drinkware manufacturing hub. Decades of clustered supply chains mean faster tooling, mature surface finishing, and a deep pool of skilled workers. Choosing a Yongkang factory means your order rides on an entire ecosystem, not a single workshop.

Manufacturing Capabilities

What 500K+ Units a Month Looks Like

We run multiple automated production lines with robotic assistance for welding, polishing and assembly. In-house tooling opens new molds quickly, while in-house printing handles laser engraving, silk-screen and heat-transfer. The result: 167+ active models, 50+ new designs introduced every year, and 500K+ units shipped monthly to 50+ countries.

OEM / ODM Services

Build Under Your Brand

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) means we produce to your approved design and put your logo on it. ODM (Original Design Manufacturer) means we develop the product and you brand it as your own. Both are available with low and flexible MOQ.
